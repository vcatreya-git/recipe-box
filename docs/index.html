The problem

Saved is not cooked.

Recipes live in screenshots, reels, and ten apps. Planning a week still means a blank note. Recipe Box is a playlist for food: a catalogue anyone can browse, a box that is yours, a week generated from that box.

How it works

Four places. One job each.

Browse is the global pool. My recipes is your library. This Week is the shape, then the shop. Profile is diet, taste, usual week, and support.
