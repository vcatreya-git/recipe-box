The problem

Saved is not cooked.

The reel is gone. Tuesday is the usual. The shop is a second of everything you already have. Recipe Box is where those recipes land — then a week that uses them, overlaps the groceries, and still has room to wander.

How it works

Four places. One job each.

Browse is the wider kitchen. My recipes is yours. This Week is the shape, then the shop. Profile is diet, taste, usual week, and support.
